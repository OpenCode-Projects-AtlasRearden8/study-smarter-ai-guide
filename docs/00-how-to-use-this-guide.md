# How to Use This Guide

*Study Smarter with AI — a friendly manual for students and lifelong learners*

## Who this is for

You. Whether you're a nursing student wrestling with pharmacology flashcards, a returning learner refreshing algebra after 20 years, a hobbyist deep-diving chess openings, or someone teaching themselves to code on the bus — this guide is for you.

One rule up front, so we're all on the same page: **this guide is about using AI to learn, not to skip learning.** Copying an AI answer into your homework is like watching someone else do push-ups and expecting to get stronger. Same effort spent, zero muscle gained. We'll show you what to do instead — and honestly, the "instead" is more fun.

## How to read it

You don't need to read straight through. Each chapter stands alone, and every chapter ends with **"Try it today"** — one small thing you can do in ten minutes. If you only take one idea from the whole guide, make it Chapter 2: *explain it back*.

A word on tone: we will never talk down to you. If something sounds complicated, that's our fault, not yours — and the [glossary](glossary.html) translates every term we use into friendly English. This guide was written by [Asdale Dynamics, LLC](README.html), in the spirit of *The Massive AI Manual*: explaining AI like a friend would.

## The one-sentence version

> Use AI as your patient, tireless study partner — the one who quizzes you, makes you explain things back, and never gets bored — while you stay the one who does the actual thinking.
